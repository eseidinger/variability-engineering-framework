Compare solar and storage options

Estimate a configuration for a site in Wasserburg am Inn using a verified reference dataset.

Reference data

The load shape is synthetic. PVGIS 2023 solar values were mapped into a derived 2024 leap-year profile. Results are estimates, not measured customer or observed 2024 data. Installation, maintenance, tax, financing, and other excluded costs are not included.
